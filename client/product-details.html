Description
Each tulip is carefully crocheted by hand, shaped and finished individually. Because every piece is handmade, tiny variations may occur — making your bouquet uniquely yours. The blooms never wilt, need zero water, and bring a gentle romantic warmth to your favorite space.
Materials
• 100% premium combed milk cotton yarn for silky touch and vibrant pastel dye.
• Flexible internal botanical craft wire for natural posability and shaping.
• Hypoallergenic organic fiberfill for plump, soft petals.
• Hand-finished raw linen ribbon and rustic jute twine.
Dimensions
• Total stem length: ~28 cm (11 inches)
• Individual bloom width: ~5.5 cm (2.2 inches)
• Bouquet diameter (Small 3-stem): ~16 cm
• Fits comfortably in standard bud vases (6–8 inch height recommended).
Care Instructions
• Keep dry. Do not immerse in water.
• Avoid rough handling or twisting stems forcefully.
• Gently remove dust with a soft makeup brush or hairdryer on cool, low setting.
• Store away from prolonged direct harsh sunlight to preserve gentle pastel yarn dyes.
Shipping & Delivery
Every bouquet is made fresh in our studio. Please allow 3–5 business days for hand-stitching and gift boxing. Once dispatched, standard delivery across India takes 3–5 business days. Real-time courier tracking is provided via email and SMS.
Returns
We take immense pride in our craftsmanship. If your piece arrives damaged or defective, please contact our studio within 7 days of delivery for a replacement or full refund. Custom personalized items with bespoke engraving cannot be returned unless transit-damaged.
