Brand Design System

BabyGurl Crochet

Complete Visual Design System & Brand Identity

BabyGurl Crochet is an independent boutique brand dedicated to high-end handmade crochet flowers, heirloom plush companions, artisanal bags, and bespoke personalized gifts. The design system creates an editorial, tactile, warm, and cozy experience that feels like discovering a talented craftsperson’s studio.

Aesthetics

Visual Direction & Guardrails

A sophisticated handmade boutique aesthetic rather than a childish craft-store or generic drop-shipping look.

✓ Visual Guardrails (DO USE)

  • Warm cream backgrounds (#FFF9F5) as the dominant airy foundation.
  • Soft dusty rose (#D96C8A) and muted blush (#F3C6D3) used with deliberate balance.
  • Deep warm cocoa brown (#3D3033) for soft, high-contrast, readable typography.
  • Subtle touches of muted gold (#D9A441) and botanical sage (#7B8F77).
  • Natural textures: linen ribbon, rustic kraft paper, washed oak wood, cotton skeins.
  • Gentle rounded corners (14px–28px) and warm ambient shadows.

✕ Guardrails (DO NOT USE)

  • Do NOT make the entire website pink or fluorescent.
  • Do NOT use bright neon or saturated synthetic colors.
  • Do NOT make it look like a childish discount craft store or cheap toy shop.
  • Do NOT use generic cookie-cutter Shopify template structures.
  • Do NOT clutter with excessive floating hearts, bows, or amateur clip art.
  • Do NOT use cold black shadows (e.g. rgba(0,0,0,0.5)); use warm cocoa tint instead.

Key Design Decisions

  • 1:1 Square Media Ratio: Ensures uniform alignment across varying device viewports.
  • Hover Elevation: Cards smoothly translateY(-4px) with custom warm rose shadow.
  • Wishlist & Quick View: Positioned non-intrusively with high accessibility contrast.
  • Yarn & Technique Snippet: Reinforces the handmade provenance on every card.

Art Direction

Product Photography Guidelines

Products are the hero of the website. Authentic handmade crochet photography must feature visible yarn texture, warm morning side-lighting, soft shadows, linen, and wooden surfaces.

Bouquet Art Direction: Warm oak table, soft window glow, raw linen bow.
Plushie Art Direction: Crinkled linen cloth, gentle nursery backdrop, wooden toy props.
Accessories Art Direction: Wooden peg hook, textured lime-wash wall, dried flora.
Gift Unboxing Art Direction: Earthy kraft paper wrapping, tea mug, jute twine tag.
