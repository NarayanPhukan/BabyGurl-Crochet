Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our custom handmade process.

How much do custom pieces cost?
Pricing depends on the size, intricacy, pattern complexity, and yarn material required. Small keychains and charms typically start around ₹249–₹499; individual flowers and plushies range from ₹499–₹1,299; and intricate pet replicas or large bespoke bouquets range from ₹1,499–₹3,500. We will provide an exact, itemized quote before you commit to anything.
How long does a custom order take?
Because each piece is designed, pattern-drafted, and crocheted stitch by stitch by human hands, preparation typically takes 5–10 business days before dispatch. If you have an urgent date (e.g. an anniversary or birthday in 7 days), please mention it in the timeline section and we will do our best to accommodate express studio crafting.
Can I send a reference image?
Yes, absolutely! Reference images make the design process much smoother. You can upload photos of real pets from different angles, sketches, Pinterest boards, screenshots, or color swatches directly in Step 2 of our request form or via WhatsApp.
Can you recreate a specific character or object?
Yes! We love bringing characters, mascots, animated icons, and symbolic sentimental objects into amigurumi crochet. We adapt 2D designs into tactile 3D crochet forms while respecting character proportions and signature colors.
Can I request a specific color?
Yes. We maintain a large studio inventory of combed milk cotton yarns in over 60 shades — including vintage pastels, earthy neutrals, vibrant botanicals, and soft muted heather tones. If you have a specific hex code or fabric sample, we can match it closely.
Can I cancel a custom order?
You can cancel freely at any time before confirming your quote and approving production. Once production begins and custom yarn has been dyed or cut, cancellations may only receive a partial refund depending on how much work has already been completed.
Do you ship custom orders across India?
Yes! We ship to all pin codes across India via insured express courier delivery (Bluedart, Delhivery, Speed Post). Real-time courier tracking numbers are sent via SMS and email once your package leaves our studio.
Can I order a custom piece as a gift?
Yes! Over 60% of our custom orders are gifts. Every custom creation comes packaged in our signature kraft box with raw-edge cream linen ribbon and dried floral accents. We can also include a free handwritten calligraphy card with your personal message and ship directly to the recipient's address with no price receipt included.
